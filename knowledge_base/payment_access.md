# Payment Help

## Payment appears unsuccessful

Check whether your payment provider reports the transaction
as successful, pending or failed.

If money was deducted but CampusHelp does not show a successful
purchase, contact support before attempting another payment.

## Information needed for payment support

Provide the course name, transaction reference, payment date
and a description of the problem.

Never provide your full card number, CVV, password or OTP.

## Duplicate charge

If you believe you were charged twice, contact support with
both transaction references.

A human support agent must investigate the transactions.

## Refund requests

Refund eligibility and exceptions must be reviewed by human support.

This knowledge base does not specify a refund eligibility period
or guarantee that a refund will be approved.
