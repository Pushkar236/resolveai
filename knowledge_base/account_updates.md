# Account Updates

## Change your display name

1. Sign in to CampusHelp.
2. Open "Profile".
3. Select "Edit Profile".
4. Update your display name.
5. Select "Save".

## Change your registered email address

Changing the registered email address requires human support
and account verification.

Do not send your password or OTP to support.

## Update notification preferences

1. Open "Settings".
2. Select "Notifications".
3. Choose your preferred notification options.
4. Save the changes.

## Restore a deleted account

Account restoration requires human review.
This knowledge base does not guarantee that a deleted account
can be restored.
