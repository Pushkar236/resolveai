# Course Access

## Open an enrolled course

1. Sign in to CampusHelp.
2. Open "My Courses".
3. Select the course you want to access.

## Newly purchased course is missing

A successfully purchased course may take up to 10 minutes
to appear in "My Courses".

Refresh the page after 10 minutes.
Confirm that you signed in using the account used for the purchase.

If the course is still missing, contact support with the course
name and purchase reference.

## Course video does not play

1. Check your internet connection.
2. Refresh the page.
3. Try another browser.

If the problem continues, provide the course name,
lesson title and displayed error message to support.

## Enrollment changes

Requests to transfer or manually restore an enrollment
require human support.
