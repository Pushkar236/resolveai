# Login Issues

## Incorrect email or password

Check that you entered your registered email address.
Passwords are case-sensitive, so check whether Caps Lock is enabled.
If you cannot remember your password, use "Forgot password".

## Account temporarily locked

After five consecutive unsuccessful login attempts,
the account is temporarily locked for 15 minutes.

Wait 15 minutes before trying again.

## Login page does not load

1. Check your internet connection.
2. Refresh the page.
3. Try a private or incognito browser window.
4. If the issue continues, contact support with the error message.

## Account-specific investigation

The knowledge base cannot determine why a specific account
is suspended or disabled. These issues require human support.
