# Password Reset

## Reset a forgotten password

1. Open the CampusHelp login page.
2. Select "Forgot password".
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. Open the password-reset email.
5. Select the reset link and choose a new password.

The reset link expires after 30 minutes.

## Reset email not received

Check your spam or junk folder.
Confirm that you entered your registered email address.
Wait five minutes before requesting another reset email.

If the email still does not arrive, contact support.

## Expired reset link

Request a new reset link from the login page.
Use the latest reset email.
